# 24 — Saved time on customer accounts

A customer can keep playing time on their account and continue it on another visit. For example, they buy a 3-hour package, play 1 hour today, and play the other 2 hours next week.

## Where the saved time comes from
- **Left over at the end of a session.** When you end a prepaid session that has a customer on it, **End session** shows *Save the 35 min left to Yaw's account*. It is ticked by default. Untick it if you'd rather refund the unused time.
- **Bought at the counter.** Go to **Customers → (customer) → Saved time → Add**:
  - choose a package, or an hourly plan plus minutes;
  - take payment (cash, MoMo, card or wallet; the member discount applies).

  The time goes straight onto the account.
- **Changed by hand.** Go to **Customers → (customer) → Saved time → Change**. Enter minutes, using minus to take time off (e.g. `-30`), and a reason such as a gift, a correction or a broken PS5.

**Add** and **Change** need the permission *customers.time*. Admins and managers have it, cashiers don't. You can tick it for any staff member in **Users & roles**.

## Continuing the time
1. On **Stations**, press **Start session** and choose the customer.
2. If they have saved time, a green box shows: **Continue from Yaw's saved time — 2 h 35 min left**. It is ticked by default.
3. Optionally type how many minutes to use. Leave it blank to use all of it.
4. Press **Continue … of saved time**. Nothing is charged, because it was paid before.

If they stop early again, the remainder is saved again when you end the session. Extending a saved-time session is charged normally.

## What the customer sees
The customer app home page shows **Your saved time** and a short history (bought, played, saved, changed).

## History
The customer page lists every change under **Saved time history**: date, what happened, plan, amount paid, minutes added or used, and the balance left. Every sale and every manual change is also recorded in the **Audit trail**.

**Refunds:** once leftover time is saved to the account, the session no longer suggests a refund for it, so the time can't be both saved and refunded.
