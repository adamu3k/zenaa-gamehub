# 21 — Chat, rewards (follow us for a bonus), game news from other sites, finding opponents

Everything here is in the **customer app** (the portal, `/portal`), with staff controls in ZENAA.

| What | Customers find it | Staff set it up |
|---|---|---|
| Find opponents (search) | **Match** tab → *Find opponents* | Nothing to set (Settings → Competitions → Find match on) |
| Chat | **Chat** tab (phones: bottom bar) | **Customers → Chat**; Settings → *Customer chat & rewards* |
| Rewards: follow us, bonus codes, promo codes | **Rewards** (phones: **More → Rewards**, and a card on Home) | **Customers → Rewards**; promo codes in **Promotions** |
| Game news from other sites | **News** tab and the Home page | **News & posters → Game news from other sites** |

---

## 1. Find opponents

On the **Match** tab, under *I want to play*, the **Find opponents** box lists players for the chosen game.

- **Search** by gamertag, name, PSN ID or EA ID (any part of it). PSN/EA IDs are searched but never shown. A player only sees them once a match is agreed.
- **Filters:**
  - **Everyone**: everyone who has played the game.
  - **My level**: within 150 rating points of you.
  - **At the centre now**: players with a running session; shown with a green ring and a green badge.
  - **Looking for a game**: players with an open request.
- Each player shows:
  - their tier and rating, and how far it is from yours, e.g. `1085 (+60)`;
  - games, wins/draws/losses, win %, the last 5 results and when they last played;
  - **You vs them**: your record against that player.
- **Buttons:**
  - **Challenge** sends a direct challenge.
  - **Accept game** shows when the player is already looking for a game.
  - **Message** opens a private chat.

Players who chose *Hide me from the public leaderboard* in their profile don't appear.

## 2. Customer chat

- **Lobby**: one room for everyone who has signed in to the app. Staff can post here as the centre, e.g. *"Tournament at 6 pm!"*.
- **Private messages** between two players. Start one by tapping a name in the Lobby (**Message …**), or with **Message** in Find opponents.
  - A small red number on **Chat** means unread private messages.
- In a chat, tap someone's message to **Challenge** them or **Report** the message.

**Staff: Customers → Chat**

- Every chat is listed; the Lobby is first. Reported messages are outlined in red and counted.
- **Delete** removes a message for everyone (it shows *Message removed*). **It's fine** clears a report.
- **Mute player**: they can still read but not write, and the app tells them to ask at the counter. **Unmute** reverses it.
- Every delete and mute is in the Audit trail.

**Settings → Customer chat & rewards**

- **Customers can chat**: on/off.
- **Blocked words**: separate them with commas. They show as `****` in messages. Outside news stories that contain one are hidden too.

Good to know:
- Players can send at most one message every 1.5 seconds, of up to 500 characters.
- The app checks for new messages every 3 seconds while the chat is open. No internet is needed, only the venue Wi-Fi or your server address.
- Customers are told that staff can read chats to keep everyone safe. That includes private chats.
- The *Chat* permission (`chat.moderate`) is given to managers and cashiers by default. Change it in Users & roles.

## 3. Rewards: follow us for a bonus, bonus codes, promo codes

Customers get **wallet credit** (free play) or a **promo code** for following your pages, or for typing a code you share. Each reward is paid **once per customer**. Bonuses appear in the wallet history as *bonus · Reward: …*.

### Follow-us bonus (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X, Snapchat, WhatsApp, website)

1. Put your page links in **Settings → Socials** first. The form then fills them in for you.
2. **Customers → Rewards → + Follow-us bonus**:
   - **Where** (e.g. YouTube) and the **link**;
   - **Title**, e.g. *"Subscribe to our YouTube"*;
   - **Bonus into their wallet**, e.g. 5.00;
   - optionally a **promo code** they get after it's approved, e.g. `SUBSCRIBER10` (create it in Promotions);
   - optionally a limit ("most customers") and an end date.
3. **Staff check before paying** is on by default, and we recommend leaving it on. No app can see who followed you on YouTube or TikTok, so somebody has to look.

**What the customer does:** Rewards → **1. Open YouTube** → subscribe → come back → type their YouTube/TikTok username → **2. I followed — get bonus**.

**What staff do:**
1. **Customers → Rewards → Claims to check** (the menu shows how many are waiting).
2. Open the page with the link icon and look for the username among your subscribers or followers.
3. Press **Pay** (the money goes into their wallet at once), or **No** with a reason the customer will see.

If you switch *Staff check* off, the bonus is paid as soon as they press the button. That is an honour system, so keep those bonuses small.

### Bonus codes

**+ Bonus code** → a word, e.g. `LIVE500`, plus the bonus. Share it on a live stream, a poster or your WhatsApp status. Customers type it under **Rewards → Have a bonus code?** and get the bonus at once, once each.

Use **Most customers** to make it *"the first 20 people"*. After 8 wrong codes, a customer has to wait 15 minutes.

### Promo codes in the app

In **Promotions**, edit a promo code and tick **Show it to customers in the app**. It then appears under **Rewards → Promo codes** with a copy button. Customers give it at the counter when paying, as before.

If a customer types a promo code into the bonus code box, the app tells them to use it at the counter instead.

**Deleting a reward** that has already paid someone switches it **off** instead, so the history stays. Customers who claimed it still see it as done.

## 4. Game news from other sites

ZENAA can show the newest stories from game news websites next to your own posts. Each story shows the picture, the site's name and **Read on IGN**, which opens the full story on that site.

1. **News & posters → Game news from other sites**.
2. Use **Quick add**: PlayStation Blog, Push Square, IGN, GameSpot or Eurogamer (all checked working). Or paste any address and press **Test**:
   - an **RSS feed** link, e.g. `https://www.pushsquare.com/feeds/latest`, or
   - the **website address**. Most news sites announce their feed and ZENAA finds it. If it says *no news feed found*, search the site for "RSS" and paste that link.
3. **Stories from other sites to show**: how many appear (default **2**; 0 switches it off).
   - **News** tab: they are mixed by date after your pinned posts.
   - **Home** page: your 2 newest posts, then up to 2 outside stories.

Notes:
- ZENAA reads the sites **once an hour** from the server. Customers' phones only load the pictures. **Check now** reads them straight away and shows *Not working: …* for any site that fails. A site that is down keeps its last stories.
- Only the title, a short summary, the picture address and the link are shown. The story itself stays on the other site.
- Stories that contain a **blocked word** (Settings → Customer chat & rewards) are hidden. We left sites with adult content out of Quick add. If you add a site yourself, check its stories first.
- Up to 8 sites.

## Quick setup (10 minutes)

1. **Settings → Socials**: paste your YouTube / TikTok / Instagram links → Save.
2. **Customers → Rewards → + Follow-us bonus**: e.g. YouTube, 5.00, *Staff check* on.
3. **Promotions**: tick *Show in the app* on a code you want customers to know about.
4. **News & posters → Game news from other sites → Quick add** PlayStation Blog and IGN.
5. Open the customer app on your phone: check **Rewards**, **Chat**, **News** and **Match → Find opponents**.
6. Print a **QR poster** (News & posters → QR posters) so customers can sign in and claim the bonus.
